# Exact installed license-notice identification

The follow-up review identifies four previously unspecified dependency declarations from their exact installed distribution files. It does not determine license compatibility, approve redistribution, or claim that all license obligations of a Linux/Windows image or every binary component have been audited.

| Distribution | Identified declaration | Preserved scope |
|---|---|---|
| itsdangerous 2.2.0 | BSD-3-Clause | One exact installed notice matches the tagged upstream text and the official documentation's named license. |
| reportlab 5.1.0 | BSD-3-Clause AND LicenseRef-reportlab-5.1.0-installed-notices | Both core notice copies plus DarkGarden GPL/font and Bitstream Vera notices. The complete texts remain in the custom reference. |
| scipy 1.18.1, reviewed Mac architecture | BSD-3-Clause AND LicenseRef-scipy-1.18.1-installed-notices | The complete wheel license with conditional native-runtime notices, plus uarray, duccfft, DOP and Qhull files. Conditional notices do not prove every named component is bundled. |
| xlrd 2.0.2 | BSD-3-Clause AND LicenseRef-xlrd-2.0.2-installed-notices | Both the bulk-work BSD notice and the earlier-work custom advertising/acknowledgement notice. Clause four of the latter is not the standard BSD-4-Clause non-endorsement clause. |

The review ledger is versioned source, not an automatically accepted hash cache. The generator compares the entire discovered notice-file set, content SHA-256 values and package version before selecting the reviewed identifier. The documented filename rules cover LICENSE/COPYING/NOTICE/AUTHORS/COPYRIGHT prefixes and named license/copying suffixes; they do not inspect arbitrary source headers or establish all binary provenance. A scan of the current 52-package closure found additional NOTICE files in pyarrow/requests, AUTHORS files in pygments/python-dateutil and COPYRIGHT in scikit-learn; the generator now records these too. None adds a file to the four reviewed distributions. SciPy additionally checks the observed platform/architecture. Added/changed/omitted/oversized/unsafe files or incomplete scans invalidate the mapping. Output keeps notice hashes in the inventory and full exact installed texts in SPDX `hasExtractedLicensingInfos`. A `LicenseRef` is the SPDX mechanism for representing licensing text without substituting an inaccurate standard identifier. All package `licenseConcluded` fields remain `NOASSERTION` because this work does not assert a complete binary-level legal conclusion. The inventory's declaration-identification count and a distribution approval are separate facts.

The authoritative comparison sources are [ItsDangerous 2.2.0 tagged notice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pallets/itsdangerous/2.2.0/LICENSE.txt), [ItsDangerous named-license documentation](https://itsdangerous.palletsprojects.com/en/stable/license/), [ReportLab's publisher release metadata](https://pypi.org/project/reportlab/5.1.0/), [SciPy v1.18.1 core notice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scipy/scipy/v1.18.1/LICENSE.txt), [xlrd 2.0.2 tagged notices](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/python-excel/xlrd/2.0.2/LICENSE), and [xlrd's licensing documentation](https://xlrd.readthedocs.io/en/latest/licenses.html). Their scope is supplemented by the pinned installed files, especially wheel-specific and font notices; a source-tree core license alone cannot replace those.

SPDX's [BSD-3-Clause matching notes](https://spdx.org/licenses/BSD-3-Clause.html), [BSD-4-Clause text](https://spdx.org/licenses/BSD-4-Clause.html) and [extracted licensing information specification](https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v2.3.1/other-licensing-information-detected/) define the identifiers/representation used here. The custom notices are preserved rather than declaring xlrd's second notice or an entire SciPy/ReportLab wheel to be one generic BSD license.

The new evidence lives under canonical `Bang-chung/20261113-k13-k18-followup/k16`. The original four-unknown reports and sealed packages are unchanged. Image-specific package provenance, notice applicability, source/notice distribution requirements and legal compatibility remain the authorized release/distribution review; this identification removes missing notice information without fabricating that approval.
